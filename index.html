Evolution · Form · Genomes

Plant diversity,
across scales.

We investigate how plant diversity arises and how genomes and plant form evolve.

We develop methods to recover and represent complex biological structures from incomplete, heterogeneous observations, making them useful for comparison and evolutionary inference.

Plant 3D Morphology

  • Reconstruction
  • Comparison
  • Evolution

We study how the three-dimensional organization of flowers evolves and relates to function, combining image-based reconstruction with quantitative comparisons on phylogenetic trees.

Plant Phylogenetics and Genomics

  • Phylogeny
  • Genomes
  • Structure
  • Evolution

We use phylogenetic and genomic evidence to understand plant relationships and evolution. Organelle genomes are a major interest, from their assembly and structural variation to what their histories reveal about plant diversification.
