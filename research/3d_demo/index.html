Reading these comparisons

The reference photographs were held out from Gaussian training. Input counts and reconstruction settings differ across methods; a common training split for all methods has not been established. These are qualitative examples, rather than a controlled ranking. Agreement in appearance does not establish geometric accuracy.

The collection and our reconstruction workflows are still growing. Get in touch to discuss the work.
